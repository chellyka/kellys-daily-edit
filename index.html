01 — Finance & Markets

Anthropic's listing looms over a wobbly AI trade.

Anthropic's IPO is set to be the biggest listing ever

An amended S-1 offers 825M shares at $70–78, up to about $64.4B, plus $26B from seven strategic buyers off-market, for up to $90.4B against SpaceX's $85.7B in June. Pricing is reportedly due Wed 18 Nov and trading Thu 19 Nov as ANT on Nasdaq, with the FT saying a price above the range is being considered. About 20% of shares are reserved for retail investors, and banks reportedly turned away orders from China and Hong Kong. The trend: AI mega-listings are now the market's main source of fresh equity. Disclosure: Anthropic makes Claude.

ETF experts: Nvidia's turmoil may leave Big Tech the winner

Experts on CNBC's "ETF Edge" say Nvidia's leadership shock and Democratic gains in Congress could favour the largest AI-linked tech stocks and rival chipmakers. Dan Ives estimates 10–20% of planned data centres may not get built, but says demand still outruns supply four to one. Nvidia ended the week at $162.85 and reports third-quarter results on Tue 17 Nov (US) under interim CEO Colette Kress. The trend: Investors are treating the shock as a reshuffle of AI winners, not the end of the trade.

02 — World & New Zealand

A three-way summit, a grounded week and a count still running.

Trump, Putin and Xi plan a joint meeting in China

The White House says the three leaders will hold a historic joint meeting at the APEC summit in Shenzhen next week, with Trump due to arrive late on Wednesday for the two-day summit. A spokeswoman said he will discuss the "reduction of nuclear weapons". Earlier reports from Al Jazeera and SCMP had treated the trilateral as unconfirmed and Beijing as lukewarm. The trend: Symbolism may outweigh substance, as analysts expect few breakthroughs. Partial article text.

BBC · Al Jazeera

A router upgrade grounded every US flight

US officials say a routine upgrade by contractor L3Harris overloaded communications routers and forced the FAA's nationwide ground stop on Friday 13 Nov. Al Jazeera reports the stop ran about four hours, with more than 9,000 flights scrapped on Friday and over 5,000 cancelled on Saturday. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy has called for $50bn to upgrade air-traffic systems. The trend: Ageing infrastructure makes even routine upgrades a national risk.

Al Jazeera

NZQA offers paper back-ups after exam outage

Students sitting NCEA exams on Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday will be able to use paper back-ups after an online outage last week cost some Level 2 English students their work. NZQA says it will cover schools' printing costs, and about 41,000 Level 1 English entries get paper access. The trend: Trust in digital assessment is now the issue.
