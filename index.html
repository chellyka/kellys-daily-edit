AI money in, rates pushing back.

Record AI capital-raising meets a bond market pricing more Fed hikes.

Anthropic's IPO would be the biggest listing ever

Range $70–78 for 825M shares (~$64.4B) plus a $26B placement: about $90.4B, above SpaceX's $85.7B July IPO. Reportedly ~6× oversubscribed. Chips sell off after Nvidia's CEO exits

Jensen Huang resigned Thursday; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. Nvidia fell 2% Friday and is down 13% on the week; AMD dropped 8.75% and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 3.43%. Reports tie the exit to US national-security concerns. The trend: key-person risk is now priced into AI stocks.

Yields climb as the Fed leans hawkish

The US 10-year hit a 4-week high near 5.17% Friday. October PPI rose 6.5% year on year, the fastest since Nov 2022, though the monthly gain of 0.3% was below forecasts. A 9 Dec Fed hike is priced in. The trend: inflation, not growth, is steering markets.

Slow-burn stories, no clean endings.

NZ waits on a count, oil waits on diplomacy, and APEC opens Monday.

New Zealand's election is still unresolved

National has 42 seats; AP and AFP report a slim two-seat majority with ACT and NZ First. About 400,000 votes stay uncounted until 27 Nov, with coalition talks after. The trend: tight blocs and a late count mean the outcome can still shift.

AP via OPB · AFP via Malay Mail

Hormuz closed ~9 months, and oil has adapted

Wood Mackenzie says the strait has been shut about nine months. Saudi and UAE producers used bypass pipelines, and the UAE is adding 1.5M bpd. Brent ~$92 on 12 Nov. The trend: workarounds are shrinking the shock.

NZ housing falls a 7th month; jobless at 5.7%

03 — Social, Fashion, Pop Culture & Celebrity

What’s trending right now.

Bond casting chatter, a Godzilla opening and bio-active skincare.

Pattinson reported as the Bond frontrunner

A Puck report by Matt Belloni, relayed by a movie-news roundup on 14 Nov, says Robert Pattinson has emerged as the quiet frontrunner for the next James Bond. Actors who screen-tested reportedly signed “test deals”; most, including Josh O’Connor and Jack Lowden, signed four-film terms, while Pattinson reportedly negotiated three films. The trend: casting leaks now drive the conversation long before any announcement. Nothing here is confirmed by the studio.

Movie News, 14 Nov

Avril Lavigne announces her first child

The singer posted black-and-white photos on Instagram announcing a baby girl, with a nod to her “Sk8er Boi” era via tiny checkerboard Vans. Reports say she did not share the baby’s name or birth date. The trend: early-2000s pop-punk nostalgia is now part of how celebrities announce life events.

Reality Tea · Reality Tea

Godzilla Minus Zero opens at No. 1 with $31.6M

The film took the 6–8 Nov US/Canada weekend, ahead of The Cat in the Hat at $20.5M, per Box Office Mojo figures reported 8 Nov. Box Office Pro notes a $63M global opening and says Warner Bros. is still in a cold streak. Johnny Depp’s Ebenezer was set to open 13 Nov after a London premiere; I have not seen its opening numbers. The trend: a subtitled-origin franchise is beating a studio family reboot.

Box Office Pro · Seat42F

SkinTok now sells: $844M in a year

TikTok Shop skincare sales hit $844.1M in the 12 months to June 2026, up 123% (New Engen, citing Charm.io). US K-beauty is up 37.2% to $2B and getting commoditised. A DermFriends newsletter also claims cryo masks hold ~85% share on TikTok (single source). The trend: growth is real but differentiation is thinning.

Gen Z dresses in the 2010s and Y2K

Marie Claire reports a 2010s revival and “uniform dressing”: repeating a small set of go-to outfits instead of chasing micro-trends. A Trendalytics-hosted piece puts Y2K at +486% year on year (a vendor claim). Both are single sources, so treat them as early signals. The trend: Gen Z is mining the recent past for style.
